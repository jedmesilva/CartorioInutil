---
name: Cartório progress, errors & seal
description: Product rules for upload feedback, user-facing errors, and document-stamp wording.
---

- Show only the active upload/authentication status; upload can show its percentage, then show the current processing update without repeated labels or explanatory filler.
- Keep operational failures generic and nontechnical in the client. Keep file-type and size validation actionable, and give users a simple next step for sharing failures.
- The circular seal wording is exactly “SELO OFICIAL DA INUTILIDADE”; do not add “DOU FÉ”.
- Place the seal wording on the upper arc with proportional letter advances rather than around a nearly closed full circle; keep the circular mark balanced with the authentication details beside it.

**Why:** The user said technical failure details confuse users and expose unnecessary information, explicitly corrected the seal copy, and reported wide word gaps, crowded phrase ends, and an oversized seal.

**How to apply:** Preserve these constraints when changing Cartório upload/authentication UI, error handling, or the shared stamp generator. If the seal copy changes, recheck the upper-arc fit and spacing.
